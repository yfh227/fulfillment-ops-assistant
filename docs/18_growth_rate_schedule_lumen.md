## Preamble

FIN-RATE-2026-01 (the Standard-tier rate card) directs Growth and Enterprise readers to
*"consult the contract, not this document."* This is Lumen Bath Co.'s contract and it is
the authoritative rate source for this account.

Where this schedule is silent, FIN-RATE-2026-01 applies as the default. Comparison columns
are informational only.

**What a Growth schedule is, in practice:** the Standard card with negotiated discounts on
volume-driven lines, a small number of client-specific additions, and — unlike Enterprise —
**no contractual service-level remedy.** Section 9 covers this distinction, which is the
one most often misunderstood internally.

**Fragile handling at receipt** covers additional inspection and careful handling for the

0.6% of units before the enhanced process and 0.1% after.

Receiving discrepancy handling follows SOP-REC-004 without variation.

**Note on escalation:** SOP-REC-004 requires immediate escalation for any *enterprise-tier*
discrepancy. Lumen is Growth tier, so ordinary thresholds apply — variance under 2% and
under 50 units is recorded and notified by end of day; at or above 2% or 50 units, hold for
written client confirmation. This is a real operational difference from the Northwind
schedule and is a frequent source of internal error.

## 9. Service levels — and what Growth tier does *not* include

**This section exists to prevent an internal error.**

Growth-tier clients receive **standard SLAs** per the company profile's tier definitions.
Standard SLAs are operational targets. They are **not** contractual commitments with defined
remedies.

| | Enterprise (e.g. Northwind) | Growth (Lumen) |
|---|---|---|
| On-time ship target | 99.2% **contractual** | 99.0% **operational target** |
| Defined credit remedy | Yes — banded, Section 9 of FIN-RATE-ENT-NWP-2025 | **No** |
| Inventory accuracy | 99.5% contractual | 99.5% operational (per SOP-INV-002) |
| Incident priority uplift | Yes — one severity level per POL-ESC-001 | **No** |
| Business review | Monthly | Quarterly |
| Account management | Dedicated Senior AM | Shared AM |

**What this means when something goes wrong:**

If Lumen experiences a service failure, Meridian has **no contractual obligation to issue a
credit.** Any credit is a **goodwill credit** under POL-FIN-003, requiring Client Operations
Manager approval at any amount, and must be recorded as goodwill rather than error.

This is materially different from Northwind, where a 97.1% on-time month automatically
generated a $6,800 contractual credit classified as error.

**The distinction matters for three reasons:**

1. **Approval path differs.** A goodwill credit needs the Client Operations Manager
   regardless of size. A contractual credit follows the standard POL-FIN-003 bands.
2. **Trend reporting differs.** POL-FIN-003 requires goodwill and error credits to be
   reported separately. Miscoding a goodwill credit as error overstates Meridian's error
   rate — the inverse of the miscoding that occurred in INC-2025-0417.
3. **Client expectation differs.** Growth clients should not be told they are "entitled to"
   a credit. They may receive one; that is a decision, not an entitlement.

**Do not tell a Growth-tier client they have an SLA credit.** They do not. Say that Meridian
will review the impact and respond — which per POL-ESC-001 is the only thing anyone below
Level 3 may say about credits anyway.

### Escalation targets

Lumen receives standard POL-ESC-001 targets with **no enterprise uplift**:

| Severity | First response | Update cadence | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 30 minutes | Hourly | 4 hours |
| S2 | 2 hours | Every 4 hours | 1 business day |
| S3 | 1 business day | Daily | 3 business days |
| S4 | 2 business days | — | 5 business days |

During weeks 46–52, S3 and S4 targets extend per the SOP-PEAK-001 triage model — S3 to 2
business days, S4 to 4 business days — **with notification required by week 45 Friday.**
Lumen is a Growth-tier client and therefore falls in the "degraded, with client notification"
band, not the protected band.

## 12. Review and change control

- **Rate review quarterly.** Either party may propose; neither may impose.
- **Volume band review:** sustained volume above 20,000 orders/month for two consecutive
  quarters triggers a renegotiation right for Lumen and a tier review by Meridian. At 13,400
  and growing 22% since signature, this is plausible within 12 months.
- **Tier review:** Growth tier spans 5,000–50,000 orders/month per the company profile.
  Lumen is not near the Enterprise threshold, but sustained growth would justify improved
  Growth-tier terms before any tier change.
- **Any rate change requires written amendment** signed by both parties.

## Billing notes

- Invoices issue on the **1st** for the prior calendar month.
- Payment terms are **net 30** unless the contract states otherwise.
- Disputes must be submitted within **60 days** of invoice date. See POL-FIN-003.
- Storage bills on peak occupancy — this is the most frequently misunderstood line
  on the invoice and the most common source of disputes.
- Peak surcharge applies to the **service date**, not the invoice date. Orders shipped
  in week 52 appear on the January invoice carrying the surcharge.

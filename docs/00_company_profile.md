# Meridian Fulfillment Co. — Company Profile

> **This is a fictional company created for portfolio and demonstration purposes.**
> All names, rates, policies, and figures are invented. Any resemblance to a real
> operation is coincidental.

---

## Overview

Meridian Fulfillment Co. is a mid-market third-party logistics provider serving
direct-to-consumer and B2B ecommerce brands.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Founded** | 2016 |
| **Headquarters** | Richmond, VA |
| **Facilities** | 3 (Richmond VA, Columbus OH, Reno NV) |
| **Total square footage** | 410,000 |
| **Headcount** | 240 (38 in client operations) |
| **Active clients** | 87 |
| **Peak daily order volume** | ~19,000 |

## Client profile

Meridian serves three client tiers, and service levels differ by tier:

**Enterprise (12 clients)** — over 50,000 orders/month. Dedicated account manager,
custom SLAs, monthly business reviews, quarterly rate reviews.

**Growth (31 clients)** — 5,000–50,000 orders/month. Shared account management,
standard SLAs, quarterly reviews.

**Standard (44 clients)** — under 5,000 orders/month. Pooled support, standard
SLAs, self-service portal.

## Services

- Receiving and putaway
- Storage (pallet, shelf, and bin)
- Pick, pack, and ship (DTC and B2B/retail)
- Kitting and light assembly
- Returns processing
- FBA prep and retail compliance labeling

## Client operations team structure

- **Client Operations Manager** (1) — team lead
- **Senior Account Managers** (4) — enterprise accounts
- **Account Managers** (9) — growth accounts
- **Client Support Specialists** (14) — standard tier, pooled queue
- **Billing Analysts** (6) — invoicing, reconciliation, disputes
- **Onboarding Specialists** (4) — new client implementation

## Known operational pain points

*Included because a realistic reference set should acknowledge what's broken.*

1. **SOP coverage is uneven.** Enterprise workflows are well documented; standard-tier
   edge cases largely live in individuals' heads.
2. **Billing disputes cluster around surcharges.** Dimensional weight and peak
   surcharges generate a disproportionate share of invoice questions.
3. **Receiving discrepancies at the Reno facility** run above the other two sites,
   cause not yet isolated.
4. **Onboarding documentation drifts.** Each Onboarding Specialist has evolved their
   own checklist variant.
5. **Ticket volume spikes** every year in weeks 46–52 without corresponding staffing.

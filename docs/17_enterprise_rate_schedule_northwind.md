## Preamble

FIN-RATE-2026-01 (the Standard-tier rate card) states that Growth and Enterprise clients
operate under individually negotiated schedules and directs readers to *"consult the
contract, not this document."*

**This is one of those contracts.** It is the authoritative rate source for Northwind
Provisions. Where this schedule and FIN-RATE-2026-01 differ, this schedule governs. Where
this schedule is silent, FIN-RATE-2026-01 applies as the default.

Comparison columns to the Standard card are included throughout. They are **informational
only** — Northwind is not billed on the Standard card and the comparison exists so that
Meridian staff answering a Northwind question do not accidentally quote the wrong number.

## 3. Storage

Billed monthly on **peak occupancy** during the billing period, not the average and not
month-end. This basis is identical to the Standard card and was **not** negotiated.

| Item | Northwind rate | Standard card | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pallet position | **$24.50 / month** | $28.00 / month | −12.5% |
| Shelf location (per cubic foot) | $0.95 / month | $1.10 / month | −13.6% |
| Bin location | $3.70 / month | $4.25 / month | −12.9% |
| Long-term surcharge (181–365 days) | +25% | +25% | — |
| Long-term surcharge (365+ days) | +50% | +50% | — |
| **Temperature-controlled pallet** | **$41.00 / month** | **n/a** | **Northwind-specific** |
| **Quarantine hold (expiry)** | **No charge, first 30 days** | **n/a** | **Northwind-specific** |

**On peak-occupancy billing:** Northwind attempted to negotiate average-occupancy billing
during contract discussions. Meridian declined. The basis is uniform across all clients
and all tiers, and POL-FIN-003 identifies it as the single most disputed invoice line
precisely because it is counterintuitive — but it is consistently applied.

Northwind's Senior Account Manager sends a peak-occupancy reminder ahead of each seasonal
build, consistent with the practice recommended in ONB-CASE-2026-058 for all tiers.

**Quarantine hold** relates to the 90-day expiry quarantine built during onboarding
(ONB-CASE-2025-114). Product automatically quarantined for approaching expiry is not
charged storage for the first 30 days, giving Northwind a markdown decision window without
a billing penalty. After 30 days, standard pallet rates resume.

**Temperature-controlled** applies to 34 SKUs at Columbus only. Richmond has no
temperature-controlled capacity, which constrains the overflow arrangement in Section 9.

**The peak surcharge is reduced to +5% but is not eliminated.** Northwind pushed hard for
zero. Meridian held at +5% on the basis that peak cost is real — SOP-PEAK-001 documents
+70 temporary heads, overtime, and the throughput constraint — and that a zero-surcharge
enterprise client shifts that cost onto the Standard tier.

**The surcharge applies to the service date, not the invoice date.** Week 52 orders bill in
January carrying the surcharge. This is the second most common dispute category per
POL-FIN-003 and Northwind disputed it in January 2026 despite it being explicit in this
schedule. Resolved as `client misunderstanding`, no credit, with a written explanation
showing the service-date basis.

**Pass-through charges carry no markup**, consistent with CAR-POL-001 Section 4. Where
Meridian's own packing error causes a DIM charge, the charge is credited as `Meridian
error` per that policy — this has occurred twice, totalling $410.

## 9. Service levels and remedies

**This section is the material difference between an enterprise schedule and the Standard
card.** FIN-RATE-2026-01 carries no SLA and no remedy. Standard-tier clients receive
standard SLAs with no contractual credit mechanism.

### Committed service levels

| Measure | Commitment | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| On-time ship | **99.2%** of orders received before cutoff ship same business day | Monthly, WMS records |
| Inventory accuracy | 99.5% location-level | Monthly, per SOP-INV-002 |
| Receiving turnaround | 95% of ASN'd inbound putaway within 2 business days | Monthly |
| Order accuracy | 99.85% (correct SKU, correct quantity) | Monthly, from returns and claims |
| Portal availability | 99.5% excluding scheduled maintenance | Monthly |

### Remedies

Credits are calculated per band and are the **sole and exclusive remedy** for service-level
failure. Consequential loss is expressly excluded.

| On-time ship attainment | Credit |
|---|---|
| ≥ 99.2% | None — commitment met |
| 98.0% – 99.19% | 2% of that month's pick/pack charges |
| 96.0% – 97.99% | 5% of that month's pick/pack charges |
| 94.0% – 95.99% | 10% of that month's pick/pack charges |
| < 94.0% | 15% of that month's pick/pack charges |

**Credits under this section are classified `Meridian error` per POL-FIN-003** and follow
that policy's approval authority. A 5% credit on a typical Northwind month is roughly
$7,200, falling in the $2,501–$10,000 band requiring VP Operations approval.

### Exclusions

No credit is due where the failure arises from:

- Client-caused delay — late ASN, incorrect SKU data, inventory unavailable
- Carrier failure after tender, per CAR-POL-001 Section 3
- Force majeure
- Orders received after the published cutoff
- Client-requested holds
- The 120-day shelf-life block preventing shipment (this is the control working correctly)

The last exclusion was added during onboarding after the shelf-life block correctly
prevented 40% of test orders from shipping. Without it, a control Northwind requested would
have generated credits against Meridian.

## 10. Northwind-specific operating requirements

Built during onboarding, documented in ONB-CASE-2025-114. Reproduced here because they
carry commercial weight.

| # | Requirement | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 120-day minimum remaining shelf life on outbound | Hard block at allocation |
| 2 | Strict FEFO, not FIFO | WMS allocation rule, client-specific |
| 3 | Lot-level traceability per order | Recorded at pick, available via portal and API |
| 4 | No lot commingling in a pick face | Enforced at putaway |
| 5 | Expiry quarantine at 90 days remaining | Automated, with client-facing report |

Requirement 4 approximately doubled Northwind's pick-face requirement at Columbus and is
the primary driver of their storage footprint. It is a client-elected cost.

Requirement 5 has prevented an estimated $180,000 of expired write-off in eight months per
Northwind's own figures.

## 11. Overflow arrangement

Northwind may place inventory at Richmond when Columbus is at capacity, by agreement, at
Columbus rates. Limitations:

- **No temperature-controlled capacity at Richmond.** The 34 temperature-controlled SKUs
  cannot overflow.
- Richmond runs 91% pallet occupancy at peak per SOP-PEAK-001 and may decline overflow in
  weeks 46–52.
- Split inventory complicates FEFO. Allocation is per facility, so the oldest lot network-wide
  may not be the oldest lot at the shipping facility. **Northwind has accepted this
  limitation in writing.**

The FEFO limitation is a genuine compromise and is the most likely source of a future
dispute. It is recorded here rather than buried in an appendix for that reason.

## 12. Review and change control

- **Rate review quarterly**, per enterprise tier standard. Either party may propose changes;
  neither may impose them.
- **Volume band review annually.** Sustained volume above 85,000 orders/month for two
  consecutive quarters triggers a renegotiation right for Northwind.
- **Business review monthly**, attended by the Senior Account Manager and Northwind's
  Director of Operations.
- **Any rate change requires written amendment** signed by both parties. Verbal agreement
  at a business review is not sufficient and has been the source of dispute at other
  accounts.

The 2026-01-14 amendment added no charge and changed no rate. It restated the service-date
